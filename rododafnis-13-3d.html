Checks before the trades start

  • Oven and microwave. The electrical plan runs an oven supply at 1.00 m and a microwave supply at 1.60 m on the kitchen’s left wall, by the balcony door. The cabinet plan has no tall oven unit, and the plumbing plan puts the kitchen table there.
  • Dishwasher. The plumbing plan provides dishwasher and washing-machine points on the right wall near the sink. The cabinet plan shows no dishwasher, and puts the washing machine on the back wall next to the fridge, about 1–1.5 m from that point.
  • Bathroom. The drawings fix the fixtures (walk-in shower, concealed cistern, basin) but not the tiles. The model uses a neutral light tile as a placeholder.

Dimensions measured off the PDF drawings at 1 px ≈ 7 mm, so expect ±3–5 cm. Furniture follows the layouts drawn on the plumbing plan.
